ZOMBCASH FIELD DOSSIER · 5 CHECKPOINTS · ON-CHAIN FIRST

THE FIVE CHECKPOINTS

Run these checks in order. Stop when evidence is incomplete, contradictory or impossible to verify.

01 / 05

IDENTITY

Verify the exact mint address on-chain. Match the token name and ticker to the mint—not to a logo or social post.

02 / 05

LIQUIDITY

Open the actual pool. Check depth, pool age, ownership and whether liquidity can be removed.

03 / 05

HOLDERS

Inspect top-holder concentration, creator wallets and linked wallets. Distinguish pool and burn addresses carefully.

04 / 05

AUTHORITIES

Review mint and freeze authorities, token extensions and upgrade controls. Understand what powers remain.

05 / 05

WALLET SIGNING

Read every transaction and message. Reject unknown approvals, blind signatures and seed-phrase requests.
