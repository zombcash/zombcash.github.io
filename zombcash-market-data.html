What automated traders usually evaluate

  • Whether a supported pool exists and has current, executable swap routes.
  • Real SOL-side liquidity, price impact, and expected slippage at the bot's trade size.
  • Recent on-chain buys, sells, unique traders, volume, and transaction cadence.
  • Token metadata, mint/freeze authority, holder concentration, and security warnings.
  • Whether the bot's own rules allow the token, pool, launchpad, and transaction route.

Public visibility does not force a bot to buy. Trading bots make independent decisions and may ignore tokens with low liquidity or activity.
