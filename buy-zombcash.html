01 // SET UP A WALLET

If you already have a Solana wallet, open its official app or browser extension. If you do not have one, install Phantom or Solflare only from its official website or verified app store. Create a wallet and store the recovery phrase offline. Never share it or enter it on a website.

02 // FUND IT WITH SOL

You need SOL in the same Solana wallet to buy tokens and pay network fees. Buy SOL through a reputable service available in your country, then withdraw it using the Solana network to your wallet. Check the address and consider a small test transfer. Keep SOL for fees.

Connect your wallet in Jupiter. The initial route is set to pay with SOL and receive ZOMBCASH. Enter the amount of SOL you want to spend; the widget estimates the tokens you may receive. 05 // HOW MUCH SHOULD I ENTER?
