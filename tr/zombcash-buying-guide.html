1. Set up a Solana wallet

Install a wallet from its official website or official app-store listing, such as Phantom or Solflare. Store the recovery phrase offline; never share it or enter it on a website.

2. Buy SOL through a service available in your country

Use a reputable exchange or on-ramp available in your country and follow its identity-verification rules. Account for fees. Payment methods and availability vary by region; use official domains and check withdrawal fees.

3. Withdraw SOL to your own Solana wallet

Copy the Solana receive address from your wallet. At the exchange, choose SOL withdrawal on the Solana network, not another chain. Verify the address and consider a small test transfer. Keep SOL for network and swap fees.

4. Verify the token before swapping

Never rely on the ticker alone: anyone can create a token with the same name. Compare the full mint address below with independent sources. If even one character differs, stop. The mint address is not the liquidity-pool address.

5. Open a swap interface and review the quote

Use a reputable Solana swap interface such as Jupiter, or find the pair on DexScreener and follow its available route. Use only the official domain. Select SOL as payment and paste the verified mint address as the token to receive. Check price impact, route, minimum received, slippage and fees.

6. Start small and verify receipt

Consider a small test swap with an amount you can afford to lose. Read the wallet request before signing. After confirmation, verify the mint and balance in your wallet or on Solscan. Never sign a transaction you cannot explain.

7. Secure the tokens and plan an exit

Avoid unknown airdrops and unsolicited support messages. Never share your recovery phrase or private key. Liquidity may be thin, slippage high, and a sell route may fail. A displayed price does not guarantee you can sell at that price.
