Why zombies never go out of style

Zombies are a stress test for society: supply chains, trust, leadership, inequality and who gets the last clean bottle of water. In ZombieCash terms, the undead are the least liquid asset: everyone wants out, nobody wants the bag, and the exit is blocked by 400 hungry customers.

The best zombie stories are not just about corpses. They ask what we preserve when systems fail: knowledge, compassion, skills, memory and cooperation. That fits ZombieCash’s apocalypse aesthetic—but this is a culture guide, not a token endorsement or a promise that digital assets outlive physical infrastructure.

Books & fiction

World War Z — Max Brooks

A fictional oral history of a global zombie war, told through survivor testimony. It explores how institutions fail, adapt and rebuild.

☣ Zombie annotation: The apocalypse has a thousand witnesses and no working customer-support desk.

Official / publisher reference ↗

The Zombie Survival Guide — Max Brooks

A mock-practical manual about preparation, tools, movement and hard choices.

☣ Zombie annotation: The original DYOR: Do Your Own Rotting. Check the exit before entering the basement.

Official / publisher reference ↗

The Girl with All the Gifts — M. R. Carey

A child in a research facility forces survivors to rethink infection, humanity and the future.

☣ Zombie annotation: Never trust a lab label reading “mostly harmless,” especially if the sample can argue.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Cell — Stephen King

A mysterious signal through mobile phones turns everyday communication into horror.

☣ Zombie annotation: Battery at 2%, network cursed, but the terms-and-conditions notification still arrives.

Official / publisher reference ↗

The Walking Dead — Robert Kirkman et al.

A long-running comic about communities after social order collapses; living people can be more dangerous than the dead.

☣ Zombie annotation: The final boss is not the horde. It is six people sharing one generator.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Films that changed the rules

Night of the Living Dead (1968) — George A. Romero

Strangers shelter in a farmhouse while the dead gather outside. The film helped define modern zombie cinema and reflects social fractures.

☣ Zombie annotation: The first community meeting ends with broken windows and bloodied minutes.

Official / publisher reference ↗

28 Days Later (2002) — Danny Boyle

A fast-moving infection empties Britain. The infected are not technically undead, but the film changed outbreak pacing.

☣ Zombie annotation: Wi-Fi is down, streets are empty, and your survival plan was “wait for delivery.”

Official / publisher reference ↗

Train to Busan (2016) — Yeon Sang-ho

Passengers are trapped on a train during an outbreak; courage and class shape who helps whom.

☣ Zombie annotation: Few seats, unlimited infection, and one passenger who should never run evacuation.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Comics & television

The Walking Dead — TV series

A survival drama about changing groups and whether people can stay humane when resources run short.

☣ Zombie annotation: The walkers are predictable. The food-allocation committee is the real boss.

Official / publisher reference ↗

The Last of Us — game and TV adaptation

A fungal pandemic devastates civilization; Joel and Ellie travel through a world of love, violence and moral compromise. The infected are not conventional undead.

☣ Zombie annotation: Spores, scarce medicine and a moral choice no poll can settle.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Video games

Resident Evil — Capcom

A survival-horror franchise built around biohazards, secretive organizations, limited resources and undead encounters.

☣ Zombie annotation: Never trust a corporation whose lab has more doors than exits.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Project Zomboid

A sandbox survival RPG about scavenging, crafting, defenses and daily needs in a collapsing Kentucky.

☣ Zombie annotation: The final boss is a bite; hunger and terrible carpentry are mini-bosses.

Official / publisher reference ↗

DayZ

An open-world survival game about scavenging, illness and deciding whether strangers are allies or threats.

☣ Zombie annotation: You found a can opener and a stranger. Guess which is more dangerous.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Dying Light

Parkour and close combat in an infected city where night changes the rules.

☣ Zombie annotation: Before jumping from a roof, check your stamina bar.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Tabletop RPGs

All Flesh Must Be Eaten — Eden Studios

A tabletop role-playing game designed for zombie-apocalypse stories across many tones and settings.

☣ Zombie annotation: The character sheet has skills, wounds and a plan. The plan dies on the first failed roll.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Zombicide — CMON / Guillotine Games

A cooperative miniature board game where survivors combine skills, gather gear and complete missions as the horde grows.

☣ Zombie annotation: Teamwork is mandatory. Arguing about who wasted the last shotgun shell is also mandatory.

Official / publisher reference ↗

Dungeons & Dragons — undead campaigns

Not a zombie-only RPG, but its undead creatures and survival scenarios make it flexible for outbreak adventures.

☣ Zombie annotation: Roll initiative. Then check whether the paladin remembers quarantine rules.

Official / publisher reference ↗

The Walking Dead Universe RPG — Free League

A tabletop RPG about survival, community and hard decisions in The Walking Dead setting.

☣ Zombie annotation: The most important resource is not ammunition; it is trust in the person holding it.

Official / publisher reference ↗
