1. Project Overview

ZombieCash ($ZOMBCASH) is a community memecoin on the Solana blockchain. Its identity combines zombie and survival-horror themes with crypto memes and community participation. The project uses this creative setting to bring people together through social content, community spaces and experimental interactive features.

ZombieCash is a meme-driven community project. It does not represent equity, a claim on company revenue, ownership of assets, a debt obligation or a right to distributions. Holding the token does not grant governance, redemption or other rights unless such rights are separately and explicitly documented by the project.

4. Operating Principles

  • Verify before acting. Publish the canonical token mint and encourage independent address checks.
  • Community participation. Prioritize accessible communication, creative contributions and transparent updates.
  • Security awareness. Protect seed phrases, inspect transaction details and distrust unsolicited links or messages.
  • Clear communication. Distinguish verified facts from proposals, experiments, estimates and future intentions.
  • No return promises. Avoid price guarantees, investment claims and language implying participation ensures profit.

5. Development Approach

ZombieCash development is community-led and subject to available resources, technical feasibility and platform constraints. Broad areas of ongoing exploration include:

  • Maintaining the official website, project documentation and multilingual access.
  • Improving community communication and publishing useful, clearly sourced updates.
  • Developing and maintaining optional interactive experiences and informational tools.
  • Improving token-verification guidance and user security education.

These are areas of work, not guaranteed milestones. No delivery date, exchange listing, partnership, product release or market outcome is promised by this document.

6. Risks and Important Disclosures

High risk: Memecoins are highly speculative and may lose some or all of their market value. Liquidity can be limited or disappear, prices can change rapidly, and you may be unable to sell when you want.
  • Market risk: Prices are volatile and can be affected by sentiment, liquidity, concentration and broader market conditions.
  • Technical risk: Blockchain networks, wallets, smart contracts, interfaces and third-party services may fail, be exploited or become unavailable.
  • Impersonation risk: Scammers may copy project names, logos, social accounts or tickers. Verify links and the full mint address.
  • Transaction risk: On-chain transfers and swaps are generally irreversible. Review recipient, token, amount and network before signing.
  • Regulatory and tax risk: Rules differ by jurisdiction and may change. Each participant is responsible for understanding applicable laws and tax obligations.

Never share a seed phrase or private key. The project will never need them to verify a token, provide support or distribute information. Do not connect a wallet to an unfamiliar site or sign a transaction you do not understand.
